# Audit events

Audit privileged or irreversible actions: role and permission changes, API-key create/revoke/use, provider/channel configuration, access grants/revocations, exports and downloads of sensitive data, deletes, impersonation, payment state changes, and manual replay/retry of external effects.

Record actor or integration identity, action, target type/id, timestamp, outcome, correlation ID, and safe context. Do not place secrets or raw PII in audit metadata.
