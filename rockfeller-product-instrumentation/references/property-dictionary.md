# Property dictionary

Use snake_case properties and stable opaque IDs. `source` is an approved enum, not a URL dump. `course_id`, `lesson_id`, and `offer_id` identify domain records. `form_variant` and `entrypoint` identify approved product variants. `provider` identifies an integration.

Do not attach names, email, phone, CPF, free text, raw URLs with query parameters, tokens, payment data, audio, transcripts, or full error payloads.
